# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The Tyrus project is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone who participates in its community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate in discussions, reviews, and other project spaces.
- Give and receive constructive feedback with an open mind.
- Focus criticism on ideas and work, not on people.
- Respect differing viewpoints, experience levels, and backgrounds.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention.
- Insults, intimidation, or repeated disruption of discussions.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Any other conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in Tyrus project spaces, including repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, discussions, and project events, as well as when someone is representing the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct that violates this policy to [tyrus@tyruslang.org](mailto:tyrus@tyruslang.org). Include a description of what happened, where it happened, and any context that may help the maintainers respond. Please do not post private reports publicly.

Project maintainers will review reports, respect the privacy of those involved as far as practical, and take action they consider appropriate to protect the community. Actions may include a warning, removal of content, temporary restrictions, or exclusion from project spaces. Maintainers will communicate the decision to the people directly involved when appropriate.

## Acknowledgment

This policy is informed by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
